# Matken website: client meeting checklist

## Ready now

- Private Project Blueprint, solar planner, request preparation, and Project Pack are complete and can be reviewed on the preview site.
- Request, invoice, and project-status screens remain safely in preview mode until a same-origin provider endpoint is approved and configured.
- The supplied media has been reviewed as candidate material only. It is not public on the website yet.

## Decisions needed to finish the public site

### Business and conversion

- Confirm the legal/public business name and the phone number already published on the website.
- Approve public email address, physical/service-area address, hours, social links, and emergency wording if any should be published.
- Confirm the primary customer action: call, WhatsApp, request form, or a combination.
- Identify the person or inbox that should receive requests and the expected response-time wording.

### Request, privacy, and customer access

- Approve a secure same-origin request endpoint, recipient, allowed fields, retention/deletion period, privacy contact, and abuse controls.
- Decide whether photos should ever be uploaded. If yes, approve the storage provider, access rules, file limits, malware review, and deletion process first.
- Confirm whether online invoice lookup/payment and private project-status links are required for launch. Both need protected server-side providers; neither is active today.

### Project stories and photos

For every image or project story selected for publication, get one clear yes/no answer for each item:

- Matken owns or has permission to publish the photo online.
- The customer/property owner has approved publication when the home, location, people, vehicle, or identifying detail is visible.
- The stated scope, equipment, capacity, outcome, and location wording are factually approved.
- Manufacturer logos and product marks are approved for use, or should be cropped/avoided.
- Approve the photo’s public caption and alt text.
